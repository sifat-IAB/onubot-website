# Simplified Onubot landing page

Approved direction: remove repetitive presentations, keep the original images,
and make the page calmer and easier to scan.

The page will follow this order:

1. Light, welcoming hero with one primary download action.
2. Three concise pastel benefit cards.
3. One patient/doctor showcase containing all ten original app screenshots.
4. Prescription OCR comparison, followed by the two existing videos together.
5. A short, prefilled bilingual chat with the full supplied conversation available.
6. Download, practical FAQ, and the existing legal/contact footer.

The connected doctor/patient graphic becomes a small supporting strip in the
showcase. Phone screens keep their aspect ratio, original pixels, white frames,
and restrained 3D depth. Screen changes are manual and keyboard accessible.
Animations stay lightweight and respect reduced motion.

Keep the fixed full-width navigation, Facebook link, deletion page and mail
endpoint. Do not change credentials or email delivery configuration.

Verify gallery navigation and audience tabs, chat expansion without losing new
messages, original asset hashes, native text readability, fixed navigation,
paired video playback, mobile overflow, legal links, and the production build.
