Submit information only when you have the authority to share it. The categories below describe

3. Why we use information
We use relevant information to authenticate accounts, review doctor applications, maintain
profiles and authorized records, provide AI and document features, arrange consultations,
support clinician-reviewed notes and prescriptions, manage bookings and enabled payment
services, deliver requested communications, and protect the platform. Technical diagnostics
help us investigate failures and operate AI services. Processing must have an appropriate legal
basis. Specific consent must be obtained where required, including for sensitive health
information, recording and optional features. We limit use to the purposes described here or
another purpose lawfully explained to you.
4. AI processing, document extraction and photos
When you use AI features, relevant questions, conversation history, profile information, selected
visit records and uploaded content may be sent to Google’s AI or cloud services to generate
answers, extract information, transcribe speech or read text aloud. Photos may be processed for
a profile or an optional creative feature you choose. Processing may take place outside your
phone. Removing a name does not necessarily make the remaining clinical information
anonymous. Using an AI feature does not authorize unrelated general-purpose model training.
Any additional purpose requires an appropriate legal basis and the required notice or consent.
Provider handling also depends on applicable contracts and settings.

5. Consultation audio and generated records
Onubot’s consultation workflow supports server-side audio recording through LiveKit and
storage in Google Cloud. This enables speech transcription and preparation of draft consultation
notes, summaries and prescriptions. Live audio and video are transmitted to provide a video call;
the recording configuration stores audio only. Advance recording notice and any required
specific consent must be obtained separately from camera or microphone permission. The
responsible clinician reviews clinical drafts before issue. The workflow attempts to remove audio
after successful clinician-reviewed processing. Recordings may remain pending if processing or
cleanup fails; immediate deletion after every call is not guaranteed.

may also be required by a binding legal request, a serious safety concern or a lawful business
transfer, subject to safeguards and notice where required. Redacted diagnostic content may still
contain sensitive information and must not be treated as fully anonymous.

7. Content standards: adult content is prohibited
Onubot does not allow adult-entertainment content, including pornography, erotic material,
sexually explicit content intended for sexual arousal, or sexual solicitation. Do not upload,
request, create or share this material through messages, images, videos, documents or AI
features. Sexual harassment, exploitation, non-consensual intimate content and any sexual
exploitation or abuse of children are strictly prohibited. Relevant, non-erotic questions about
sexual or reproductive health and lawfully shared medical information are not adult
entertainment; share only what is necessary for a legitimate healthcare purpose and respect
privacy and consent. Report prohibited content to admin@anchorblock.vc. Access may be
restricted or content handled under the applicable terms and law.
8. Permissions, background processing and local storage
Camera permission supports image capture and consultations; microphone permission
supports voice input and calls. File and photo pickers let you select uploads. Notifications
support app alerts, which may reveal information on a lock screen. Internet access and
foreground Data Sync services allow active chat or document work to continue while the app is
minimized. The app does not include GPS walking tracking or calorie-estimation tracking,
although IP-based technical information may indicate a general location. Settings, sessions and
working copies may be cached on the device. Permission controls depend on the operating
system; refusing a permission may limit the related feature.
9. International processing
Cloud and communications providers may process information outside Bangladesh, including in
the United States and other service locations. The configured Langfuse service uses a United
States endpoint. A global AI endpoint does not guarantee storage in a particular country.
Applicable transfer restrictions, contractual safeguards and any requirement to keep a local copy
must be addressed before a transfer. Agreeing to this Policy does not waive mandatory
protections for sensitive health information. Contact us for information about applicable
processing locations and safeguards.
10. Retention and deletion limits
Information is retained for the stated service purpose and any applicable clinical, legal, security,
financial or dispute-related need. A single retention period does not apply to every record.
Account information may be needed while your account remains active; clinical records may
require a different lawful period. Audio awaiting transcription, failed jobs, diagnostic logs and
Langfuse traces require separate retention and cleanup arrangements. Where backups exist,

11. Your choices and privacy rights
Where applicable law provides these rights, you may request access, a copy, correction or
deletion of your information, ask for processing to be restricted, object to processing, or
withdraw consent for consent-based processing. Withdrawal does not undo earlier lawful
processing and may make a related feature unavailable. You can adjust device permissions and
notifications and use available profile or chat controls. Clearing a conversation does not
necessarily remove consultation records, logs, provider traces or the account itself. Important
AI-assisted clinical information should be reviewed by a clinician rather than relied on as an
automated decision.
12. Requesting account or data deletion
Email admin@anchorblock.vc with the subject "Onubot account/data deletion". Identify the
account and specify the information you want deleted. Do not send passwords, OTPs, card PINs
or unnecessary medical attachments. We may verify your identity or authority in a proportionate
manner before acting. We will explain any lawful retention or limits, including records a clinician
must independently retain and copies already shared or downloaded. Requests will be handled
within applicable legal requirements. Signing out or uninstalling the app is not a deletion
request. If an in-app deletion option is unavailable in your version of Onubot, use the email
address above to request deletion.
13. Children and another person’s health information
Accounts are intended for adults aged 18 or over. An authorized parent, guardian or clinician
may provide a child’s information for a lawful care purpose, subject to appropriate authority and
consent. Permission to provide a child’s health information does not create an unrestricted
independent child account. Do not submit another person’s confidential information without
permission or another legal basis. Contact us if a child has registered independently or
information was provided without the required authority. The adult-content prohibition in
section 7 concerns prohibited material; it does not prohibit lawful adult users or legitimate
healthcare questions.
14. Security, advertising and purpose limits

services must process readable content to provide AI, transcription or support. Do not assume
that the entire service is end-to-end encrypted or that every local cache is encrypted. No system
is completely secure. Report a suspected exposure to admin@anchorblock.vc; applicable breach-
assessment and notification duties remain. This Policy does not authorize selling health
