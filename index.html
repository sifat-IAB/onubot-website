A LITTLE MORE TO KNOW

worth knowing.

How does the prescription scanner work?

The scanner is designed to help organize handwritten prescription information into readable medicine names and instructions. Handwriting recognition can make mistakes, so always check the original prescription with a doctor or pharmacist before taking medicine.

Can I book in-person and video doctor visits?

The app brings together online and in-person doctor booking options. Available doctors, consultation times, fees, and locations are shown in the app.

How much do consultations cost?

Fees depend on the doctor and appointment option. Check the app for current fees and availability before booking.

Is the assistant on this website a real AI?

This website includes a scripted, interactive product preview. It is not connected to a live AI service, does not store your messages, and cannot diagnose conditions or prescribe treatments. Consult a qualified professional for medical advice.

Clinical & informational disclaimer

Onubot helps with health information, prescriptions, and appointments. It does not replace a clinician’s diagnosis, treatment, or emergency care.

Doctor network & medical standards

Consultations are provided by independent clinicians. Check a doctor’s qualifications and BMDC registration before booking.
