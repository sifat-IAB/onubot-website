Privacy Policy

Onubot · Anchorblock Technology

1. Responsibility and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Anchorblock Technology, House 432, Road 6, Avenue 5, DOHS, Dhaka 1215, Bangladesh, handles personal information through Onubot’s patient and doctor services. For privacy questions or requests, email admin@anchorblock.vc. For urgent platform support, call +8801400655308; this is not an emergency medical service. This Policy describes our handling of information. Treating clinicians and external service providers may have separate legal responsibilities. This notice is not blanket consent for every use of personal information, nor is it a certification that all legal and technical safeguards have been independently audited.

2. Information we collect or receive

The information we handle depends on the features you use. You may provide information directly; an authorized clinician may create consultation records; and sign-in or infrastructure providers may supply account and technical information. Guest conversations may still use an app-generated authentication or session identifier. Health information may include physical or mental-health details, a child’s information, and sensitive information entered in a message. Submit information only when you have the authority to share it. The categories below describe information we handle, not permission to access everything on your device.

Account and identity

Name, email, phone, profile photo, sign-in identifiers and verification status.

Health profile

Age or birth date, gender, height, weight, BMI, blood group, conditions, medicines and allergies you provide.

Chats and uploads

Messages, voice-derived text, selected images/PDFs, prescriptions, reports, extracted text and corrections.

Consultations and visits

Doctor, date, time, mode, appointment status, shared clinical details, audio, transcripts, notes, summaries and issued prescriptions.

Doctor verification

Professional and clinic details, BMDC information, qualifications and submitted identity or credential documents.

Transactions, if enabled

Amounts, references, status, receipts, refunds and relevant doctor payout details.

Technical and communication data

Device/app information, IP and connection data, security signals, notification tokens, diagnostic logs and support messages.

Preferences and local cache

Language, theme and notification choices, local session information and working copies used by the app.

3. Why we use information

We use relevant information to authenticate accounts, review doctor applications, maintain profiles and authorized records, provide AI and document features, arrange consultations, support clinician-reviewed notes and prescriptions, manage bookings and enabled payment services, deliver requested communications, and protect the platform. Technical diagnostics help us investigate failures and operate AI services. Processing must have an appropriate legal basis. Specific consent must be obtained where required, including for sensitive health information, recording and optional features. We limit use to the purposes described here or another purpose lawfully explained to you.

4. AI processing, document extraction and photos

When you use AI features, relevant questions, conversation history, profile information, selected visit records and uploaded content may be sent to Google’s AI or cloud services to generate answers, extract information, transcribe speech or read text aloud. Photos may be processed for a profile or an optional creative feature you choose. Processing may take place outside your phone. Removing a name does not necessarily make the remaining clinical information anonymous. Using an AI feature does not authorize unrelated general-purpose model training. Any additional purpose requires an appropriate legal basis and the required notice or consent. Provider handling also depends on applicable contracts and settings.

5. Consultation audio and generated records

Onubot’s consultation workflow supports server-side audio recording through LiveKit and storage in Google Cloud. This enables speech transcription and preparation of draft consultation notes, summaries and prescriptions. Live audio and video are transmitted to provide a video call; the recording configuration stores audio only. Advance recording notice and any required specific consent must be obtained separately from camera or microphone permission. The responsible clinician reviews clinical drafts before issue. The workflow attempts to remove audio after successful clinician-reviewed processing. Recordings may remain pending if processing or cleanup fails; immediate deletion after every call is not guaranteed.

6. Who receives information

Information may be shared with your selected or otherwise authorized clinicians, people you choose to share it with, and authorized operational personnel who need it for the stated service or support purpose. The providers below receive information relevant to their functions; their inclusion does not mean every type of data is sent during every use. Appropriate disclosures may also be required by a binding legal request, a serious safety concern or a lawful business transfer, subject to safeguards and notice where required. Redacted diagnostic content may still contain sensitive information and must not be treated as fully anonymous.

Google / Firebase / Google Cloud

Authentication, cloud records and files, backend processing, notifications and Crashlytics diagnostics.

Google AI / Vertex AI / speech services

Relevant health questions, context, selected documents or audio/text for generation, extraction, transcription and read aloud.

LiveKit Cloud

Live audio/video, connection information and server-side consultation audio recording.

Serper and public search/source websites

Search terms for medical evidence or medicine information; queries are derived from the request and should minimize private details.

Google Workspace / Gmail email delivery

Recipient and message information for verification, welcome, appointment and service emails.

Google Calendar, when you choose export

Appointment details included in the calendar action; your calendar’s sharing settings apply.

EPS or the disclosed payment provider, if enabled

Payment-entry data handled by the gateway and transaction references used by Onubot.

Device speech and platform services

Audio or read-aloud text may be handled by the speech engine you use, subject to device/provider settings.

7. Content standards: adult content is prohibited

Onubot does not allow adult-entertainment content, including pornography, erotic material, sexually explicit content intended for sexual arousal, or sexual solicitation. Do not upload, request, create or share this material through messages, images, videos, documents or AI features. Sexual harassment, exploitation, non-consensual intimate content and any sexual exploitation or abuse of children are strictly prohibited. Relevant, non-erotic questions about sexual or reproductive health and lawfully shared medical information are not adult entertainment; share only what is necessary for a legitimate healthcare purpose and respect privacy and consent. Report prohibited content to admin@anchorblock.vc. Access may be restricted or content handled under the applicable terms and law.

8. Permissions, background processing and local storage

Camera permission supports image capture and consultations; microphone permission supports voice input and calls. File and photo pickers let you select uploads. Notifications support app alerts, which may reveal information on a lock screen. Internet access and foreground Data Sync services allow active chat or document work to continue while the app is minimized. The app does not include GPS walking tracking or calorie-estimation tracking, although IP-based technical information may indicate a general location. Settings, sessions and working copies may be cached on the device. Permission controls depend on the operating system; refusing a permission may limit the related feature.

9. International processing

Cloud and communications providers may process information outside Bangladesh, including in the United States and other service locations. The configured Langfuse service uses a United States endpoint. A global AI endpoint does not guarantee storage in a particular country. Applicable transfer restrictions, contractual safeguards and any requirement to keep a local copy must be addressed before a transfer. Agreeing to this Policy does not waive mandatory protections for sensitive health information. Contact us for information about applicable processing locations and safeguards.

10. Retention and deletion limits

Information is retained for the stated service purpose and any applicable clinical, legal, security, financial or dispute-related need. A single retention period does not apply to every record. Account information may be needed while your account remains active; clinical records may require a different lawful period. Audio awaiting transcription, failed jobs, diagnostic logs and Langfuse traces require separate retention and cleanup arrangements. Where backups exist, deletion may follow their rotation and legal requirements. No unestablished automatic deletion deadline is promised. Contact us for information about the retention arrangements that apply to your records.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

Where applicable law provides these rights, you may request access, a copy, correction or deletion of your information, ask for processing to be restricted, object to processing, or withdraw consent for consent-based processing. Withdrawal does not undo earlier lawful processing and may make a related feature unavailable. You can adjust device permissions and notifications and use available profile or chat controls. Clearing a conversation does not necessarily remove consultation records, logs, provider traces or the account itself. Important AI-assisted clinical information should be reviewed by a clinician rather than relied on as an automated decision.

12. Requesting account or data deletion

Email admin@anchorblock.vc with the subject "Onubot account/data deletion". Identify the account and specify the information you want deleted. Do not send passwords, OTPs, card PINs or unnecessary medical attachments. We may verify your identity or authority in a proportionate manner before acting. We will explain any lawful retention or limits, including records a clinician must independently retain and copies already shared or downloaded. Requests will be handled within applicable legal requirements. Signing out or uninstalling the app is not a deletion request. If an in-app deletion option is unavailable in your version of Onubot, use the email address above to request deletion.

13. Children and another person’s health information

Accounts are intended for adults aged 18 or over. An authorized parent, guardian or clinician may provide a child’s information for a lawful care purpose, subject to appropriate authority and consent. Permission to provide a child’s health information does not create an unrestricted independent child account. Do not submit another person’s confidential information without permission or another legal basis. Contact us if a child has registered independently or information was provided without the required authority. The adult-content prohibition in section 7 concerns prohibited material; it does not prohibit lawful adult users or legitimate healthcare questions.

14. Security, advertising and purpose limits

We use authentication, account permissions and access controls to protect information. Some services must process readable content to provide AI, transcription or support. Do not assume that the entire service is end-to-end encrypted or that every local cache is encrypted. No system is completely secure. Report a suspected exposure to admin@anchorblock.vc; applicable breach-assessment and notification duties remain. This Policy does not authorize selling health information, behavioral advertising based on that information, or unrelated secondary use without the required legal basis and notice. A new purpose cannot be concealed as general service improvement.

15. Updates, questions and complaints

We will give appropriate notice of material changes and seek new consent where required. For support or privacy concerns, contact Anchorblock Technology, House 432, Road 6, Avenue 5, DOHS, Dhaka 1215, Bangladesh; email admin@anchorblock.vc or call +8801400655308. This does not prevent a complaint to a competent authority or another remedy available under applicable law. For medical emergencies, seek appropriate emergency medical care rather than waiting for platform support.
